# Northlake Facilities and Meter Register

Packet 0.3 | Issued 2026-09-14 | Fictional customer

This register describes the requested setup. Every building appears once, under the company that owns its site, and lists every meter that serves it. Installation and ingestion acceptance are pending.

## Customer decisions

- **Ownership:** Campus, Cedar Row and Thermal Plant are separate EEM companies. A building exists once, in the company that owns its site, and holds every meter that serves it.
- **Thermal plant boundary:** The plant company contains only the Central Plant: its utility services, production meters and controller. The chilled-water and steam meters at served buildings belong to the university, with the plant as provider.
- **Cedar Row metering:** Master-metered by building with owned submeters: one electric and one water submeter per apartment plus house meters. The utility bills the property, never a resident.
- **Names and areas:** Buildings keep the names the customer uses (Science Center, Library); every document and manifest uses the same name and the scenario areas.
- **Student Center thermal service:** Steam only; chilled-water service is not part of the current inventory.
- **Weather:** Northlake University owns the shared KSAC and KSMF references. All buildings use KSAC observations; KSMF is the secondary and forecast source.
- **Deduct meters:** Kitchen, pool-fill and cooling-tower deduct submeters are read monthly by hand; the input path into EEM is a Stage 2 decision.
- **Stormwater:** Campus building water accounts include stormwater ERUs; irrigation, fire-service, Central Plant and Cedar Row accounts have no stormwater charge.
- **Solar:** Northlake retains RECs under HOS-PPA-2024. Keep exported energy distinct from total generated energy.
- **Overlapping measurements:** Library historian values and meter readings represent the same service; do not add them together.
- **Rollup boundary:** Campus interval totals cover the four interval-metered buildings (Science Center, Student Center, Lakeview Residence Hall, Recreation and Aquatics Center); they are not the entire campus utility total.
- **FIG variants:** Seven format cases reuse one destination measurement in separate restored runs. Never ingest all variants together as independent consumption.
