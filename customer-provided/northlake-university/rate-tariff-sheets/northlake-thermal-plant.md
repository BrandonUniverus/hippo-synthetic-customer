# Rate Sheet — Northlake Thermal Plant (Internal Cost Allocation)

Provided by: Northlake University Finance + Thermal Plant. Synthetic/illustrative
values for setup. The thermal plant is **university-owned** and runs as its own
operating company, Northlake Thermal Plant — there is no external utility account
for chilled water or steam. It produces chilled water (cooling) and steam
(heating) and **allocates its operating cost** to the buildings it serves, using
internal cost centers and submetered consumption.

**Effective Jan 1, 2024 – Dec 31, 2025**

## Chilled Water Cost Allocation (NTP-ALLOC-CHW)

| Charge | Basis | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumption | per ton-hour | $0.168 |
| Demand | per ton per month (peak) | $9.50 |

Served (sub-metered) buildings: Science Center and Recreation and Aquatics
Center (hourly ton-hours and tons).

## Steam Cost Allocation (NTP-ALLOC-STEAM)

| Charge | Basis | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumption | per klb (thousand lb steam) | $14.20 |

Served buildings: Science Center, Admin Hall, Library, Student Center, Lakeview
Residence Hall, Recreation and Aquatics Center.

**Notes for setup:**
- These behave like monthly "bills" against internal **cost centers** (one per
  served building, six in all), not utility accounts — please roll them up
  alongside utility costs.
- The plant holds its own utility accounts at the Central Plant: Valley Electric
  (chiller power, VED-TOU-PRI), Sierra Gas (boiler fuel, SGU-GL-1) and River City
  (makeup water, RCU-W-TIER and RCU-S-VOL). Its electricity and gas tie its
  emissions to delivered thermal energy.
- Cooling demand is seasonal and peaks on summer afternoons.
- **Open:** confirm the allocation rule for steam-only buildings.
