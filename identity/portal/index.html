Campus energy

Where the campus gets its energy

Northlake buys electricity, gas and water from the utilities that serve Sacramento, makes its own chilled water and steam at the Central Plant, and generates power from rooftop solar and its own wind farm. Every source is metered, and every meter reports to Energy Hippo.
