# Transfer Price Sheet — Ridgeline Wind (Wind Generation)

Provided by: Northlake Thermal Plant accounting with University Finance.
Synthetic/illustrative values for setup. Ridgeline Wind is **our own** wind
company: the Thermal Plant bought it on July 1, 2024 with university capital
funding. Ten 660 kW turbines (6.6 MW) stand in the Montezuma Hills near Rio
Vista, about fifty miles southwest of campus, and the site doubles as the
Mechanical Engineering department's Wind Energy Research Lab.

The plant buys all of Ridgeline's output at an internal transfer price. Nobody
mails this bill: we want EEM to **generate** the monthly statement from the ten
turbine pad meters.

## Agreement: Ridgeline Internal Energy Transfer (RWL-XFER-2024)

**Effective Jul 1, 2024 – Dec 31, 2025**

| Item | Basis | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfer energy | per kWh metered at the turbine pads | $0.058 |
| Research lab credit | per month, for the lab's use of the site | −$1,250.00 |
| Renewable energy certificates (RECs) | ownership | **Northlake retains** |

The monthly statement = transfer energy (sum of the ten turbine meters) − the
research lab credit. It posts as a journal between the plant and Ridgeline: no
payment leaves the plant company.

**Account:** SYN-RWL-G-0000001, one transfer agreement for the whole farm, at
Ridgeline Turbine Field. Turbine meters SYN-RWL-M-0110001 to SYN-RWL-M-0110010;
each carries its position tag, RWL-WTG-01 to RWL-WTG-10.

**Notes for setup:**
- The statement is priced on the **turbine pad meters**, not on Valley
  Electric's interconnection meter: collection losses and station service are
  Ridgeline's, not the plant's.
- July 2024 was the first month. Ridgeline accounting made that statement by
  hand (sample enclosed with the bills); EEM's first generated statement should
  match it to the cent.
- Valley Electric separately credits the farm's export and moves the credit to
  the Central Plant's account (VED-BCT, on the Valley Electric sheet). The
  transfer price is the plant paying its own company; the bill credit is real
  money from the utility. They are different things; report both.
- WTG-07 was down from February 11 to April 22, 2025 with a failed gearbox. Its
  meter kept reporting zero, so those statements are lower; that is not an
  estimate and needs no adjustment.
- **Open:** confirm whether the research credit continues after the 2025 grant
  year.
