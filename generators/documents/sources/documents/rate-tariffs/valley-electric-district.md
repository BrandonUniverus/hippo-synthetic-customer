# Rate Tariff Sheet — Valley Electric District (Electric)

Provided by: Northlake University Finance. Synthetic/illustrative values for
setup. Provider: Valley Electric District (VED), municipal electric distribution.

## Schedule: Time-of-Use General Service (VED-TOU-GS)

Applies to our general-service electric accounts (listed in the notes below).
Demand is billed on the highest 15-minute average kW in the period. Power factor
below 0.95 on lab-heavy buildings can add a reactive-power adjustment.

**Effective Jan 1, 2024 – Dec 31, 2024 (FY2024)**

| Charge | Basis | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy — On-Peak | per kWh (summer Jun–Sep, weekday afternoons) | $0.241 |
| Energy — Part-Peak | per kWh | $0.168 |
| Energy — Off-Peak | per kWh | $0.112 |
| Demand | per kW (max 15-min) | $18.40 |
| Facility charge | per month | $64.00 |
| Power factor adjustment | when PF < 0.95 | as applicable |
| Public purpose program | rider | as applicable |
| Utility users tax | % of charges | as applicable |

**Effective Jan 1, 2025 – Dec 31, 2025 (FY2025 rate change, VED-TOU-GS-FY25)**

| Charge | Basis | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy — On-Peak | per kWh | $0.258 |
| Energy — Part-Peak | per kWh | $0.179 |
| Energy — Off-Peak | per kWh | $0.118 |
| Demand | per kW (max 15-min) | $19.75 |
| Facility charge | per month | $67.50 |

**Seasons:** Summer = June–September (on/part/off-peak periods). Winter =
remaining months (part/off-peak only; no on-peak).

in the period.

fixture charge and no usage.
