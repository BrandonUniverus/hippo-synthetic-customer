# Rate Tariff Sheet — River City Utilities (Water / Sewer / Stormwater)

Provided by: Northlake University Finance. Synthetic/illustrative values for
setup. Provider: River City Utilities (RCU), municipal multi-service. One account
and one monthly statement carry up to **three services**. Only water is metered;
sewer volume is derived from metered water; stormwater is a flat area-based fee.
Irrigation and private fire service lines are billed on their own accounts.

**Effective Jan 1, 2024 – Dec 31, 2025**

| Charge | Basis | Rate |

| Meter charge — 2 in | per month | $96.00 |

| Usage Tier 1 | per kgal, 0–50 | $4.85 |
| Usage Tier 2 | per kgal, 50–200 | $6.40 |
| Usage Tier 3 | per kgal, over 200 | $8.15 |

## Sewer — Volumetric on water (RCU-S-VOL)

| Charge | Basis | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base charge | per month | $28.50 |
| Volume | per kgal of metered water | $7.95 |

Apartments are capped at a winter-average volume for the sewer charge. Our own
sewer-deduct submeters reduce the sewer volume billed at the Student Center
(kitchen), Recreation and Aquatics Center (pool fill), Cedar Row Common House
(pool fill) and Central Plant (cooling tower); we read them by hand each month.

## Stormwater — Impervious area (RCU-SW-ERU)

| Charge | Basis | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ERU charge | per ERU per month | $11.20 |

ERU = equivalent runoff unit (impervious area). The bundled accounts for Admin
Hall, Science Center, Library, Student Center, Lakeview Residence Hall and
Recreation and Aquatics Center carry a stormwater charge; the Cedar Row and
Central Plant bundled accounts have water and sewer only.
